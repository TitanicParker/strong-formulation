Counsel entry point

One history. Several possible breaches.

The repository should no longer be read as one negligence allegation with one factual hinge. July 2017, the Protest if proved, the discharge communication, the Podiatry handoff, the 2018 treatment-state evidence and the 2023 safety episode are separate expert questions with separate dependencies and separate causation analyses. A further longitudinal question now asks whether later evidence was repeatedly used locally without equivalent global revision of the founding neurological–foot boundary.

Master architecture

Do not force the case into one chain

A — Initial formulation: was the integrated feet–gait–tone problem adequately neurologically assessed before the feet were left “Not clear” and operationally separated?

B — Decision after notice: if the Protest occurred, did reasonable care require reopening before closure?

C — Discharge communication: if the Protest occurred, did the founding synthesis fairly convey the provenance of “Not clear” to the GP?

D — Handoff: even if Podiatry was appropriate, was there a sufficiently specific neurological question or return mechanism?

E — 2018 failure to update: once Neurology's own treatment-state evidence crossed the earlier boundary, did reasonable care require reopening the relationship?

F — Later updating: at what later threshold, if any, should the working formulation have materially changed?

G — 2023: was the later pain–mobility–falls–psychological safety state adequately managed as a separate contemporaneous risk object?

Each route must be decided on the information available at that date. Losing one route does not necessarily decide the others.

The founding 2017 question

The GP sent an integrated feet/gait/tone presentation. Neurology documented marked rigidity/bradykinesia, a strikingly abnormal gait, a lower-limb appearance suggestive of dystonic posture, pes planus and hammertoes, while the painful feet were ultimately left compatible with “completely separate”, possible peripheral neuropathy and “Not clear”.

Was the neurological assessment directed to the feet–gait–tone relationship sufficient before that endpoint was reached?

The authority of “Not clear”

The founding communication projects unusually high specialist authority: prolonged inpatient assessment, detailed examination, imaging and laboratory work, Complex Case Meeting, senior neurological input, neurogenetic reasoning and a forward treatment plan.

Within that communication, “Not clear” can read as the considered endpoint of extensive specialist inquiry.

The question is not simply whether uncertainty was permissible. It is what work had been done to earn that uncertainty.

Conditional Protest route

The patient's account is that he expressly challenged the completeness of the foot assessment before discharge. That remains a factual question requiring proof.

If proved, it creates a separate decision-after-notice issue and may also alter the provenance of the discharge communication.

If the Protest occurred, later readers received “Not clear” without the contemporaneous fact that the adequacy of the underlying inquiry had just been challenged.

Podiatry is not the same question

Podiatry was a real, pre-existing and appropriate pathway for genuine structural foot disease. But the surviving handoff is generic and does not identify a neurological question concerning dystonia, tone, gait/loading or a defined return mechanism.

The counterfactual is not “no Podiatry.” It is whether reasonable care required neurological and mechanical findings to be reintegrated.

The 2018 independent backstop

Professor Healy records dramatic motor improvement with low-dose procyclidine and rasagiline. During withdrawal, contemporaneous evidence records tension building, restless feet and gait deterioration. In April 2018 the treating neurologist himself records that the first thing changing rapidly was the return of the outside-left-foot phenomenon, with broader worsening off procyclidine.

This does not prove mechanism. It creates a fresh decision threshold.

Even if July 2017 “Not clear” was initially reasonable, once the treating neurologist recorded rapid treatment-state alteration of the focal foot phenomenon, did reasonable neurological practice require reopening the original relationship? If not, why not?

The mechanical target

Podiatry repeatedly documented and mechanically offloaded the recurrent left fifth-MTPJ site, with clawed toes and later pronating foot type. That establishes genuine mechanical pathology.

It does not establish that the mechanical forces were neurologically independent.

Was the structural lesion mechanically independent, neurologically influenced, or mixed?

Local updating, global non-revision

The later evidence was not simply ignored. Dystonic features informed phenotype, procyclidine informed treatment, Podiatry treated focal mechanics, OFF-state reasoning explained pain/shuffling, Stalevo altered treatment, pregabalin altered pain and Sinemet responsiveness in the feet was later acknowledged.

The longitudinal question is whether those observations were repeatedly used for local clinical purposes without equivalent global revision of the original neurological–foot relationship.

Selective utilisation without integrative revision: did the record keep learning facts without adequately learning what those facts meant together?

Formulation-dependent uncertainty

The repository distinguishes unavoidable uncertainty from a possible structural form of uncertainty in which relevant observations are compartmentalised and used narrowly rather than assembled into a revised model.

This is not proof of confirmation bias or lock-in. It is an expert question.

Did later evidence cause the founding formulation to be reconsidered, or did the founding formulation principally govern how the later evidence was interpreted?

Ownership of the relationship

The claimant need not prove that every corn, callus, deformity or pain episode was itself neurological. Mixed mechanisms may be the point.

Neurology did not have to own every foot lesion. The question is whether reasonable practice required Neurology to retain ownership of the unresolved relationship between motor state, loading, structure and pain.

If the disorder was multifactorial, where was the process that distinguished the neurological, mechanical and structural contributions?

Why routes survive independently

The defence could defeat the Protest and still face the 2018 question. It could establish that July “Not clear” was initially reasonable and still face a failure-to-update allegation. It could establish that Podiatry was appropriate and still face an integration/handoff question. It could establish correct Parkinsonism and still face a feet–motor formulation question.

The case is no longer binary.

Causation must be route-specific

For every alleged breach, ask what should have happened, what would probably have been discovered, what management would probably have changed, when, and which later harms would probably have been avoided or reduced.

No breach automatically carries the whole later history.

The repository presently identifies breach candidates more clearly than it proves the damages counterfactual.

Master expert exercise

For each route require the expert to identify: earliest breach date; exact information then available; standard required; expected action; breach opinion; counterfactual management; probable causal consequence; and what cannot now be determined.

Then ask the separate longitudinal synthesis question: were the successive observations adequately integrated into a revised model of the neurological–foot relationship?

Separate 2023 question

2023 remains analytically separate. Severe pain, major walking restriction, high-dose pregabalin, prior falls-risk information and directly observed stumbles create a contemporary safety question. The extensive later reassessment and improved mood/pain are important defence facts.

Was the combined pain–mobility–falls–psychological safety state adequately recognised and managed as one contemporaneous risk object?

Current counsel posture

The repository now identifies one longitudinal history containing several potentially independent negligence questions plus a longitudinal synthesis issue. The Protest materially strengthens the 2017 case if proved, but April 2018 may provide an independent clinical backstop because the treating neurologist's own note records rapid treatment-state alteration of the focal outside-left-foot phenomenon. Later evidence then raises the separate question whether local clinical use occurred without adequate global revision of the founding boundary. Each alleged breach requires its own neurological standard and its own causal counterfactual.

One clinical history. Several decision points. Several possible breaches. Local updating may have occurred without global revision.
