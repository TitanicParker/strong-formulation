A forensic model of a longitudinal decision history

Before the Protest: omission. After the Protest: decision. After the synthesis: durable partition.

The patient did not contest a separation he already understood. He said the feet had not been properly dealt with and the neurological work could not therefore be complete. The discharge synthesis then supplied the durable architecture that followed.

Five steps

1

Integrated phenotype

Feet, gait, pathological tone, toe-clawing and function enter Neurology together.

2

Incomplete neurological work

Neurology identifies severe young-onset Parkinsonism, a dystonic-looking lower-limb sign and structural feet, but no identified substantial post-discovery remapping of the feet appears before closure.

3

Established Protest

Before closure, the patient says in substance that the feet have not been properly dealt with and Neurology cannot therefore be finished. The neurologist answers Podiatry.

4

The synthesis constructs the partition

The feet become possibly separate, possibly neuropathic, “Not clear”, and Podiatry. “Markedly divorced” travels; the Protest does not.

5

Later biology crosses the boundary

Procyclidine response/withdrawal, recurrent fifth-MTPJ loading, GP reintegration and later dopaminergic relief repeatedly test the partition without an identified global reconstruction.

Why the moment matters

The Protest did not merely turn a known unresolved relationship into a contested one. It forced an unfinished part of the phenotype back into view. From that point the treating neurologist had to decide whether to reopen the neurological question or proceed without doing so. The record shows the latter route.

1

Direct notice

The treating neurologist knew before closure that the patient said a material part of the presenting phenotype had not been properly worked through.

2

Decision

The answer was Podiatry. No neurological reopening and no defined neurological handover question or return mechanism is identified.

3

Documentary construction

The final synthesis preserves dystonic, structural and PRKN-related material but allocates the feet principally to a separate/peripheral-neuropathy/Podiatry channel.

4

Later evidential reliance

The resulting documentary history later acquires evidential authority when historical letters and shared-understanding language are invoked in response to challenge.

The patient said the feet had not been investigated. The neurologist answered Podiatry. The discharge synthesis then supplied the intellectual architecture that made that answer look medically complete.

The most revealing join

Podiatry repeatedly identified and mechanically offloaded the recurrent fifth-MTPJ site. Neurology documented that the outside-left-foot phenomenon changed rapidly with neurological treatment state.

Podiatry knew where it was. Neurology knew it changed with neurological treatment. The missing piece was the join.

The biology updated. The document did not.
