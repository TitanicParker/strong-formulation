10-minute route

From incomplete phenotype to durable partition.

The central question is not whether July 2017 contained legitimate uncertainty. It is what happened after the patient directly identified that a material part of the presenting phenotype had not been properly worked through before closure.

The corrected causal spine

integrated phenotype→neurological discoveries→incomplete foot examination→Protest: “you cannot be finished”→Podiatry answer→no neurological reopening / no defined handover question→discharge synthesis constructs separate-or-neuropathic foot channel→Protest omitted→“As per above” makes partition portable→later biological and mechanical boundary crossings→local updates, no identified global reconstruction
The hinge

Before the Protest: omission. After the Protest: decision.

The patient did not protest a separation he already understood himself to be challenging. He said the feet had not been properly dealt with and the neurological work could not therefore be complete. The neurologist answered Podiatry. The record identifies no neurological reopening and no defined neurological handover question or return mechanism.

The patient said the feet had not been investigated. The neurologist answered Podiatry. The discharge synthesis then supplied the intellectual architecture that made that answer look medically complete.

What the synthesis does

It does not simply “leave an unresolved relationship.” It recognises the dystonic-looking lower-limb sign, painful structural feet and possible PRKN/PARK2, then allocates the feet principally to a separate/peripheral-neuropathy/Podiatry channel ending “Not clear.” It strengthens the patient-derived distinction into “markedly divorced” and omits the Protest.

The synthesis does not lose the feet. It decides what explanatory pathway they are allowed to inhabit.

What the case does not require

It does not require proof that every structural foot lesion had one neurological cause, proof that Neurology should have known the exact mechanism in July 2017, proof that every later treatment decision was wrong, or proof of private motive.

Why the later record matters

The biology repeatedly crossed the partition.

Procyclidine response and withdrawal changed gait, tension and feet together. The April 2018 clinic note records rapid return of an outside-left-foot phenomenon during withdrawal. Podiatry independently documented a recurrent fifth-MTPJ loading problem and repeatedly offloaded it mechanically. Primary care repeatedly regenerated the integrated feet–tone–gait formulation. Later OFF-related pain/shuffling and dopaminergic relief again crossed the boundary.

Podiatry knew where it was. Neurology knew it changed with neurological treatment. The missing piece was the join.

The biology updated. The document did not.

The evidential consequence

The hospital’s longitudinal record cannot serve as complete and self-sufficient proof of the material decision pathway because the durable continuity begins only after the patient’s direct warning that the phenotype was incompletely worked through had been omitted from the founding synthesis.
