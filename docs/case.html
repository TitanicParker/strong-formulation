10-minute route

From whole referral to directional synthesis.

The central question is not whether Neurology was wrong to focus intensely on severe young-onset Parkinsonism. It is what happened when, after days of that work, the patient restored the underweighted opening problem of the GP referral before closure.

The corrected causal spine

integrated GP referral→striking neurological discoveries→asymmetric clinical weighting→days of diagnostic/treatment commitment→patient restores paragraph one→Podiatry answer→no identified neurological reopening→discharge synthesis joins all elements directionally→separate / neuropathic / “Not clear” / Podiatry foot channel→Protest omitted→“As per above” makes arrangement portable→later boundary crossings absorbed locally
The hinge

The patient restores the referrer’s original problem.

The referral begins with bilateral foot problems, callouses/corns and abnormal gait, then explains the neurological signs that make the whole presentation urgent. Neurology’s intense focus on the striking syndrome may initially be understandable. The decisive event is that, before closure, the patient expressly says the feet have not been properly dealt with.

He is not introducing a new problem. He is putting paragraph one back into play.

What the synthesis does

The synthesis is not evidence that nobody ever joined the relevant facts. It contains the dystonic-looking lower-limb sign, painful structural feet, possible PRKN/PARK2, possible neuropathy, the patient’s phenomenological distinction and the treatment strategy. It is the directional join: Parkinsonism/PRKN/treatment receive extensive explanatory force while the painful feet are made compatible with a separate/peripheral-neuropathy/Podiatry route ending “Not clear.”

The discharge synthesis decides what the restored problem is allowed to mean.

What the case does not require

It does not require proof that the neurologist literally failed to read the referral, that every foot symptom had one neurological cause, that levodopa had to be started, or that private pride or deliberate concealment is established.

Why timing matters

Commitment pressure without mind-reading.

By the Protest, substantial diagnostic, senior and therapeutic commitment has accumulated around severe young-onset Parkinsonism, possible PRKN/PARK2 and a cautious non-levodopa strategy. The record does not prove vanity or embarrassment. It does show an objective professional-coherence problem: reopening paragraph one now means acknowledging that an elementary part of the referred problem remains insufficiently worked through despite days of specialist work.

Why the later record matters

The founding relationship remains unusually durable.

Procyclidine response and withdrawal changed gait, tension and feet together. The April 2018 clinic note records rapid return of an outside-left-foot phenomenon during withdrawal. Podiatry independently documented and mechanically offloaded a recurrent fifth-MTPJ site. Primary care repeatedly regenerated the integrated feet–tone–gait formulation. Later OFF-related pain/shuffling and dopaminergic relief again crossed the boundary.

The later question is no longer “where was the join?” The founding synthesis had already joined the facts. The question is: where was that founding arrangement globally reconstructed?

The evidential consequence

The hospital’s longitudinal record cannot serve as complete and self-sufficient proof of the material decision pathway merely by displaying continuity, because the founding synthesis itself is the object requiring explanation: it was produced after the patient restored the underweighted first paragraph of the referral and it determined what the known facts were allowed to mean going forward.
