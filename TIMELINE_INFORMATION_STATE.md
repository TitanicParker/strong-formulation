This timeline follows the information state in the corrected order. The central change is that the pre-Protest state is **not** described as an already-recognised unresolved foot relationship. It is an **incompletely examined phenotype**. The Protest forces the feet back into view; the durable separation comes afterwards in the discharge synthesis.

## The corrected causal spine

> **Integrated phenotype → neurological discoveries → incomplete foot examination → Protest: “you cannot be finished” → Podiatry answer → no neurological reopening / no defined handover question → discharge synthesis constructs separate-or-neuropathic “Not clear” foot channel → Protest omitted → “As per above” makes the partition portable → later mechanical and pharmacological evidence repeatedly crosses the boundary → local updates without identified global reconstruction.**

## The most revealing join

> **Podiatry knew where it was. Neurology knew it changed with neurological treatment. The record does not identify the point at which those facts were formally joined.**

## The longitudinal safety proposition

> **The biology updated. The document did not.**

That is how fragmentation became dangerous: later clinicians could encounter fragments of a condition whose most important information existed in the relationships between those fragments.
