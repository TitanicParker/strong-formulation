01 / Causal spine

The corrected sequence

  1. 01

    Integrated phenotype

    Feet, gait, tone, toe-clawing and function enter Neurology together.

  2. 02

    Incomplete neurological work

    Neurology identifies major neurological and structural findings, but no identified substantial post-discovery remapping of the feet appears before closure.

  3. 03

    The Protest

    The patient says, in substance: the feet have not been properly dealt with; you cannot be finished.

  4. 04

    Decision

    The neurologist answers Podiatry. No neurological reopening or defined handover question is identified.

  5. 05

    Durable partition

    The synthesis constructs the separate/peripheral-neuropathy/“Not clear” foot channel, strengthens “markedly divorced,” omits the Protest and carries the result forward.

02 / Documentary function

The synthesis does not lose the feet.

It decides what explanatory pathway they are allowed to inhabit.

The document preserves the dystonic-looking lower-limb sign, structural feet and possible PRKN/PARK2, but does not use the dystonic/foot material to integrate the PRKN/Parkinsonian construction. The feet remain neurologically possible principally through a different route: peripheral neuropathy.

The patient said the feet had not been investigated. The neurologist answered Podiatry. The discharge synthesis then supplied the intellectual architecture that made that answer look medically complete.

03 / Later tests

The biology repeatedly crossed the partition.

Procyclidine response and withdrawal changed gait, tension and feet together. Podiatry repeatedly documented and mechanically offloaded the recurrent fifth-MTPJ site. Primary care repeatedly regenerated the integrated formulation. Later OFF-related pain/shuffling and dopaminergic relief again crossed the boundary.

Podiatry knew where it was. Neurology knew it changed with neurological treatment. The missing piece was the join.

The biology updated. The document did not.
