## The longitudinal pattern

The timeline is not a list of missed diagnoses. It shows something more specific:

> **Integrated presentation → specialist uncertainty → Protest → omission → ambiguous ownership → split information streams → repeated boundary-crossing evidence → local treatment updates → no identified global reconstruction → survival of “Not clear.”**

The most revealing join is the 2017–2018 pair:

> **Podiatry knew the left fifth MTPJ was a recurrent loading problem and built an insole to offload it. Neurology knew the outside-left-foot phenomenon changed rapidly with neurological treatment state. The record does not identify the point at which those facts were formally joined.**

That is the information-state failure in miniature.
