01 / Causal spine

The corrected sequence

  1. 01

    Whole referral

    The GP begins with painful abnormal feet and gait, then explains why pathological tone, toe-clawing, movement difficulty and functional decline require neurological explanation.

  2. 02

    Asymmetric specialist weighting

    The striking young-onset Parkinsonism/PRKN problem receives extensive development; the painful abnormal feet receive comparatively limited direct evaluation in the surviving record.

  3. 03

    Referral return

    The patient says, in substance: the feet have not been properly dealt with; you cannot be finished. He restores the originating problem rather than introducing a new one.

  4. 04

    Decision after restoration

    The answer is Podiatry. No neurological reopening or defined handover question is identified after the original problem is expressly back in view.

  5. 05

    Directional founding join

    The synthesis contains the structural, dystonic, Parkinsonian and PRKN material, but arranges it so painful feet can travel through separate/peripheral-neuropathy/“Not clear”/Podiatry territory while the neurological disease architecture is elaborated elsewhere.

02 / Documentary function

The synthesis is the join.

The question is not whether the pieces were joined. It is why the completed join points where it points.

The document preserves the dystonic-looking lower-limb sign, structural feet, Parkinsonism and possible PRKN/PARK2. It also renders the patient’s limited symptom distinction as “markedly divorced,” leaves the causal relationship “Not clear,” and gives the painful feet an operational Podiatry-compatible destination.

The patient restored the referrer’s original problem. The discharge synthesis then decided what that restored problem was allowed to mean.

03 / Later tests

Later evidence repeatedly crossed the founding join.

Procyclidine response and withdrawal changed gait, tension and feet together. Podiatry repeatedly documented and mechanically offloaded the recurrent fifth-MTPJ site. Primary care repeatedly regenerated the integrated formulation. Later OFF-related pain/shuffling and dopaminergic relief again crossed the boundary.

The founding join existed: it was the discharge synthesis. The later missing operation was the re-join.

The biology updated. The documentary architecture did not visibly reconstruct itself around those updates.

04 / Shortest formulation

Whole referral. Asymmetric weighting. Referral return.

Directional synthesis. Durable inheritance. Later boundary crossings without identified global reconstruction.
