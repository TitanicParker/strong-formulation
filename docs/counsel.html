Counsel entry point

Material from which drafting can begin

The controlling proposition is documentary: continuity cannot independently authenticate the completeness of a founding information state if the material contest was omitted before that continuity developed.

Aggravating factor 1

Omission from an intended authoritative account after direct notice

The treating neurologist received a direct patient challenge to the adequacy of the proposed disposition while the final synthesis was still being produced. The synthesis then omitted that challenge while preserving patient-understanding material supportive of the chosen plan. Because the same synthesis became the portable historical account and was later relied upon as evidence of the reasoning and shared understanding, the omission did not merely make the record incomplete: it materially altered the evidential representation of the decision process itself.

The aggravation is not simply that the Protest is missing. It is that the document later relied upon to prove the decision process omitted the event that most directly qualified that process.

Four linked features

  1. Direct notice: the Protest converted unresolved uncertainty into known contested uncertainty before closure.
  2. Still-open synthesis: the challenge existed while the authoritative account was still being formed through discharge and later finalisation.
  3. Directional selection: supportive patient-state information travelled; the decisive contrary patient-state did not.
  4. Later evidential authority: the documentary history was later invoked as evidence of reasoning and shared understanding.

Self-protecting effect: the fact capable of challenging the documentary account was absent from the documentary account, and the documentary account was later used to prove that the historical process had been adequate.

Five load-bearing defects

  1. Integrated presentation becomes an unresolved “Not clear” relationship.
  2. The Protest makes that unresolved state expressly contested before closure.
  3. The authoritative portable synthesis omits the fork but preserves a compatible plan/knowledge-state.
  4. Later boundary-crossing evidence accumulates without an identified global reconstruction.
  5. The historical letters and longstanding understanding are invoked in 2025 as part of the answer to the historical challenge.
