Counsel entry point

Material from which drafting can begin

The controlling proposition is narrower than “an unresolved relationship was contested.” The patient identified an incompletely examined phenotype before closure; Neurology did not reopen it; the discharge synthesis then constructed and transmitted the durable partition.

Controlling hinge

Before the Protest: omission. After the Protest: decision.

The patient did not say that Neurology had wrongly separated the feet from Parkinson’s disease. He said, in substance, that the feet had not been properly dealt with and the neurological work could not therefore be complete. The neurologist answered Podiatry. No neurological reopening and no defined neurological handover question or return mechanism is identified.

The patient said the feet had not been investigated. The neurologist answered Podiatry. The discharge synthesis then supplied the intellectual architecture that made that answer look medically complete.

Four linked features

  1. Integrated phenotype: feet, gait, tone, toe-clawing and function entered Neurology together.
  2. Direct notice: before closure, the patient said the feet had not been properly worked through.
  3. Documentary construction: the synthesis then allocated the painful feet to a separate/peripheral-neuropathy/Podiatry channel ending “Not clear,” while strengthening “markedly divorced.”
  4. Later evidential authority: the documentary history later became part of the evidence invoked to support historical reasoning and shared understanding.

Five load-bearing defects

  1. The pre-Protest state is an incompletely examined phenotype, not an already-recognised unresolved relationship.
  2. The Protest creates the decision point: reopen the neurological question or proceed without doing so.
  3. The record shows Podiatry as the answer, without identified neurological reopening, defined handover question or return mechanism.
  4. The discharge synthesis constructs the durable partition and omits the event that exposed the phenotype as unfinished.
  5. Later mechanical and pharmacological evidence repeatedly crosses the partition without an identified global reconstruction.

What the synthesis actually does

It preserves the dystonic-looking lower-limb sign, structural feet and possible PRKN/PARK2, but does not use the dystonic/foot material to integrate the PRKN/Parkinsonian construction. The feet remain neurologically possible principally through a different route: peripheral neuropathy.

The synthesis does not lose the feet. It decides what explanatory pathway they are allowed to inhabit.

Boundary-crossing evidence

Procyclidine response/withdrawal changed gait, tension and feet together. The April 2018 note records rapid return of an outside-left-foot phenomenon. Podiatry repeatedly documented and mechanically offloaded the recurrent fifth-MTPJ site. Later GP and dopaminergic evidence again crossed the boundary.

Podiatry knew where it was. Neurology knew it changed with neurological treatment. The missing piece was the join.

Issues requiring expert evidence

Clinical mechanism of foot pain/dystonia; significance and limits of treatment-response evidence; appropriate interpretation of structural and neurological coexistence; what mapping/reassessment would ordinarily be expected after a dystonic lower-limb sign and direct pre-discharge notice; and the clinical significance of PRKN/PARK2 in the differential.

Issues requiring documentary-production evidence

Document drafting/discharge/finalisation chronology; any post-Protest neurological reassessment; any neurological question actually handed to Podiatry; any defined return criterion; provenance of supportive patient-knowledge assertions; and any document said to globally reconstruct the founding partition.

Central evidential consequence

The hospital’s longitudinal record cannot serve as complete and self-sufficient proof of the material decision pathway because the durable continuity begins only after the patient’s direct warning that the phenotype was incompletely worked through had been omitted from the founding synthesis.

The biology updated. The document did not.
