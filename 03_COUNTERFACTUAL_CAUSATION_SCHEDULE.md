## Causation rules for this repository

1. Later improvement is evidence, not automatic proof of the earlier counterfactual.
2. The expert must state the probable competent pathway, not merely that another pathway was possible.
3. The plaintiff must isolate harm caused by delay from harm caused by the underlying Parkinsonian and/or structural condition.
4. Documentary defects require proof of a downstream clinical consequence before they become a damages theory.
5. Loss-of-chance language is not to substitute for proof of probable causation without specific Irish legal advice and expert evidence.
6. Each defendant is assessed only on information actually or constructively available to that defendant at the relevant time.
