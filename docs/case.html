10-minute route

One case, not 38 essays.

The question is whether documentary continuity can independently authenticate a pathway when the founding portable synthesis omitted the material Protest that preceded closure.

The causal spine

integrated presentation→patient-derived separation strengthened→Protest→known contested uncertainty→fork omitted→portable plan + compatible knowledge-state→later boundary tests→local updates, no identified global reconstruction→2025 challenge→historical continuity invoked

What the case establishes

The record entered with an integrated problem and an expressly unresolved relationship. Before closure, the patient protested the adequacy of the foot disposition. That Protest is an established case fact. The portable founding synthesis omitted it while preserving the selected branch and compatible knowledge-state. Later evidence repeatedly bore on the same boundary, and the 2025 response invoked documentary continuity and shared understanding.

What it does not require

It does not require proof that every structural lesion was caused by Parkinsonism, proof that every clinical entry was false, proof of a single exclusive mechanism, or proof of private motive.

Audit discipline

The Protest is not expected to appear in the account whose omission is under examination. Its absence from that portable account cannot be used to downgrade the event; the absence is the documentary defect being tested. Failure to prove proposition X is not affirmative proof of the opposite proposition.
