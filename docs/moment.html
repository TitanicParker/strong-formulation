The Moment the Record and the Patient Diverged

The Protest occurred before closure while the authoritative synthesis was still being formed. The selected branch and supportive patient-state survived into the portable record. The decisive contrary patient-state did not.

Before

Integrated gait/feet/tone problem. Severe young-onset Parkinsonism. A gait appearance suggestive of dystonic posture. Structural foot disease. The relationship: “Not clear.”

E002 · E004 · E005

Direct notice

Established case fact: before closure, the patient directly challenged the adequacy of the foot disposition because the presenting feet had not been adequately returned to.

“full NCT … send me out on bald tires?”

The Protest converted residual uncertainty into known contested uncertainty. It was material information known to the person responsible for the disposition.

E008

Selective portable account

The synthesis retains “Not clear,” “markedly divorced,” a firm forward plan, exceptional cognition/insight and “good understanding.” It omits the Protest.

E005 · E006 · E007

Supportive patient-state information travelled; the decisive contrary patient-state did not.

Why this is aggravated: this was not an incidental missing detail from an ordinary note. The omission occurred in the account intended to preserve the material reasoning, patient understanding and forward plan, and that documentary history later acquired evidential authority. The fact capable of challenging the account was absent from the account later used to support the adequacy of the historical process.
