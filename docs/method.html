Method

Persuasive because auditable

Claim typing

Derived claims are tagged as fact, clinician assertion, patient-state, documentary analysis, record-audit inference or audit question. Analysis is not silently promoted into primary fact.

Provenance

Each evidence item carries document, date, service, source status and `CLIN-` range. The Protest is treated as an established project fact while the surviving extract's retrospective patient-authored provenance remains explicit.

Negative proof discipline

Failure to prove deliberate concealment is not proof of innocence. Failure to prove one exclusive mechanism is not proof that separation was reasonable. Absence of an identified reconstructive document remains an audit question open to later production.

Privacy

The canonical forensic file is preserved unchanged. Pages publishes only minimally necessary extracts and derived data. Direct identifiers are excluded from the generated site artifact.

Validation

`scripts/validate.py` checks JSON, `CLIN-` existence/range ordering, profile/claim references, locked quote text, internal links, date format and common privacy patterns.

Build

`scripts/build_site.py` creates `_site/` from `docs/` and `data/` only. The canonical medical transcription is not copied into the Pages artifact.
