# Join the Dots — Longitudinal Reconstruction

**Status:** CONTROLLING RECONSTRUCTION LAYER — 9 September 2026

This file sits between chronology and argument.

Its purpose is not to introduce a new allegation. It reconstructs the clinical information state at successive thresholds and asks what happened to the unresolved neurological–foot proposition as new evidence accumulated.

The governing discipline is:

> **No individual observation is asked to prove more than it proves. The analytical force arises from asking what the information state became after each observation, what remained unresolved, and what the next decision or document did with that unresolved proposition.**

This is the repository's formal **join-the-dots layer**.

---

## 1. The method

At each threshold ask only three questions:

> **What was now known?**

> **What remained unresolved?**

> **What happened to the unresolved proposition next?**

The method deliberately separates:

- evidence sufficient to prove a mechanism;
- evidence sufficient to require reconsideration;
- evidence sufficient to justify a clinical disposition;
- and evidence sufficient to support later claims of diagnostic continuity.

The repository should resist atomised reasoning of the form:

> **Event A has a benign explanation. Event B has a benign explanation. Event C has a benign explanation. Therefore there is no larger pattern.**

The proper longitudinal question is:

> **Which explanatory model accounts for the whole sequence with the fewest undocumented transitions?**

## 2. 29 June 2017 — integrated referral state

### What was known

The GP referral presented one integrated problem: painful abnormal bilateral feet, abnormal gait and neurological concern including increased tone and impaired movement.

### What remained unresolved

The relationship between the foot pathology and the abnormal motor state had not yet been neurologically determined.

### What happened next

Neurology became responsible for examining the relationship between the presenting foot problem, gait and the emerging motor syndrome.

The starting state was therefore not:

> **Parkinson's plus an unrelated foot complaint.**

It was:

> **an integrated feet–gait–tone problem referred for neurological explanation.**

---

## 3. July 2017 bedside state — neurological syndrome and abnormal feet coexist

### What was known

The record identified severe young-onset Parkinsonism, marked rigidity/bradykinesia, striking gait, a lower-limb appearance **“somewhat suggestive of a dystonic posture – NOT FOOT DROP”**, an **“almost dystonically unusual”** walk, and painful structurally abnormal feet.

### What remained unresolved

The relationship between the dystonic-looking lower limb / abnormal gait and the painful abnormal feet remained uncertain.

### What happened next

The final synthesis developed sophisticated Parkinsonian, recessive and PRKN/PARK2 reasoning while the foot relationship remained **“Not clear”** and compatible with being separate/peripheral.

This creates the first internal comparator:

> **high-resolution neurological synthesis versus relatively low-resolution documented interrogation of the disputed foot–lower-limb relationship.**

Or:

> **the synthesis may have outrun the examination.**

---

## 4. July 2017 closure — uncertainty becomes operational

### What was known

The service had recognised both a serious neurological syndrome and significant foot pathology.

### What remained unresolved

Whether the painful structural foot state was neurologically influenced, mechanically secondary to the movement disorder, independent, or mixed.

### What happened next

The uncertainty was not merely left epistemically open. It acquired an operational consequence through discharge and Podiatry disposition.

The repository's distinction is:

> **epistemically uncertain, operationally committed.**

Podiatry could be entirely appropriate for structural pathology while still not answering the unresolved neurological ownership question.

The key question is therefore:

> **Was the structural lesion referred for treatment while ownership of the neurological–mechanical relationship itself remained unallocated?**

---

## 5. DATscan — support for Parkinsonism, not closure of aetiology

### What was known

The DAT request described a very young man with bilateral Parkinsonism, atypical features and an **“almost dystonically unusual”** walk, and stated suspicion of an autosomal-recessive Parkinsonian disorder.

The radiology report described findings consistent with idiopathic Parkinson's disease or Lewy body dementia.

The treating neurologist regarded the abnormal result as expected and LBD as clinically inappropriate.

### What remained unresolved

The specific aetiology of the Parkinsonian syndrome.

### What happened next

The treating record continued to develop recessive neurogenetics and specific PRKN/PARK2 reasoning.

Therefore:

> **the clinicians' own post-DAT conduct shows that they did not treat the scan as resolving the disorder into idiopathic Parkinson's disease.**

The DATscan supports the existence of a Parkinsonian disorder. It does not bridge the later aetiological discontinuity by itself.

---

## 6. September 2017 — Healy adds diagnostic specificity

### What was known

Professor Daniel Healy personally reviewed the patient and recorded that he **“probably has Park II.”** He recommended PARK II testing first and a broader panel if negative.

### What remained unresolved

Genetic confirmation and the precise relation of the dystonic lower-limb/foot phenotype to the Parkinsonian disorder.

### What happened next

The later neurological record preserved the specific hypothesis: PARK2/PRKN was recorded as what **“we/Prof Healy suspect is the cause.”**

At this stage the aetiological state was therefore not merely generic Parkinson's disease.

It was:

> **Parkinsonism with a specifically suspected PRKN/PARK2 cause.**

---

## 7. Late 2017 treatment response — the boundary is crossed biologically

### What was known

Procyclidine and related neurological treatment produced substantial improvement in motor state. During withdrawal, contemporaneous material records increasing tension, restless feet and deterioration of walking.

### What remained unresolved

Whether and how the longstanding foot problem was mechanistically connected to the neurological state.

### What happened next

The observations were clinically relevant but did not, on the surviving record, produce a visible global reconstruction of the founding foot–neurological relationship.

The correct threshold is not:

> **Did this prove dystonia caused the foot lesion?**

It is:

> **Did this create a reason to reopen a relationship already left unresolved?**

---

## 8. April 2018 — independent reopening threshold

### What was known

The treating neurologist recorded that during procyclidine withdrawal the **first thing** changing, and changing rapidly, was the return of the outside-left-foot phenomenon, alongside broader worsening and greater physical tension/restlessness.

### What remained unresolved

The mechanism remained unproved.

### What happened next

The key expert question becomes:

> **Even if the July 2017 “Not clear” position was initially reasonable, did this treatment-state dechallenge require the original foot/neurological relationship to be reopened?**

This is a lower threshold than proving causation.

The distinction should remain explicit throughout the repository:

> **evidence sufficient to require investigation is not the same as evidence sufficient to prove mechanism.**

---

## 9. Podiatry — increasingly precise mechanical information

### What was known

Podiatry repeatedly documented real focal structural pathology, including recurrent fifth-MTPJ callus/corn, clawed toes, debridement/enucleation and offloading.

### What remained unresolved

Whether the mechanical target was independent of, secondary to, or materially influenced by the abnormal neurological loading/motor state.

### What happened next

The mechanical stream became increasingly well characterised.

The reconstruction question is:

> **Where was that increasingly precise mechanical information clinically rejoined to the neurological stream?**

Podiatry's appropriateness does not answer that question.

---

## 10. 2019 primary care — explicit reintegration appears outside Neurology

### What was known

Primary care explicitly linked the feet with increased tone and abnormal gait related to Parkinson's disease.

### What remained unresolved

Whether this relationship had been formally reconstructed within the specialist neurological formulation.

### What happened next

The record contains evidence that the integrated relationship could be stated plainly in primary care, but no identified corresponding global specialist reconstruction of the founding partition is yet visible.

This is important because it shows that the integrated proposition was not conceptually inaccessible.

---

## 11. 2022–2025 — repeated local updating

Later Neurology documented, among other things:

- persistent morning foot limitation despite major restored mobility;
- clawing and OFF dystonia;
- pain and gait worsening in OFF states;
- toe curling persisting separately from pain;
- Sinemet improving the experience of foot pain;
- and recent Sinemet CR providing **“profound relief.”**

These observations do not prove one mechanism.

What they demonstrate is repeated acquisition of information capable of bearing upon the original neurological–foot relationship.

The longitudinal audit question is:

> **What was each observation allowed to change?**

The repository describes the pattern as:

> **selective utilisation without integrative revision.**

Or:

> **local updating, global non-revision.**

The purpose of this reconstruction layer is to make that proposition testable rather than rhetorical.

## 12. Later reconsideration — reconsideration of what?

The later record contains genuine reconsideration. That must be preserved.

Current-state questions were revisited, including:

- OFF state;
- pain modulation;
- neuropathy;
- Fabry disease;
- toe curling;
- levodopa response;
- medication balance.

But a separate question remains:

> **Was the original 2017 clinical object reconstructed?**

That would require reassembling the genealogy of:

> **integrated referral → dystonic-looking lower limb → painful abnormal foot → founding “Not clear” partition → treatment-state crossings → recurrent focal mechanical pathology → later tone/gait reintegration → OFF/toe-curling phenomena → dopaminergic pain response.**

Thus:

> **“There was later reconsideration” does not by itself answer “Was the founding relationship reconstructed?”**

Read [`INTEGRATIVE_OWNERSHIP_AND_OBJECT_PRESERVATION.md`](INTEGRATIVE_OWNERSHIP_AND_OBJECT_PRESERVATION.md) for the deeper object-preservation question:

> **Can every later category be locally correct while the original integrated clinical object nevertheless disappears?**

That layer distinguishes ownership of the individual components from ownership of the relationships between them, and asks the practical systems question:

> **Who owned the arrows?**

It also adds a second audit test alongside the missing-transition test used below:

> **Where is the reconstruction?**

## 13. Diagnostic continuity — continuity of what?

The aetiological record changes materially.

The sequence is:

> **2017 Galway: recessive PRKN/PARK2 seriously entertained**

> **2017 Healy: probably PARK II**

> **later Monaghan: PARK2/PRKN is what “we/Prof Healy suspect is the cause”**

> **later record: iPD increasingly becomes the operative baseline**

> **25 March 2025: categorical statement that the service's “belief and understanding” is idiopathic Parkinson's disease**

> **same rebuttal: Healy invoked as an early validating Parkinson's expert**

> **same rebuttal: “In essence we have known that [the patient] has Parkinson's.”**

The documentary operation is significant because the specific PARK II content does not travel forward with Healy's authority.

The shortest formulation is:

> **Healy's authority survived; Healy's proposition did not.**

This creates a controlling continuity question:

> **Continuity of what?**

There may be continuous recognition and treatment of a Parkinsonian disorder.

That does not establish stable continuity of the specific aetiological formulation.

The broader category **“Parkinson's”** can contain both PARK II and iPD and therefore preserve apparent continuity by moving upward in abstraction.

Thus:

> **continuity of care is not continuity of formulation; continuity of wording is not continuity of evidential content.**

## 14. The missing-transition test

The repository should now routinely identify clinically material transitions that a competing explanation requires.

Examples include:

> **PRKN/PARK2 seriously suspected → [documented exclusion or revised reasoning?] → categorical iPD**

> **unresolved neurological–foot relationship → [integrative handoff/reassessment?] → continuing separate management streams**

> **2018 treatment-state crossing → [formal reopening?] → later continuation of inherited architecture**

> **later current-state reconsideration → [historical reconstruction?] → retrospective continuity claim**

A missing transition does not prove misconduct.

It creates a precise evidential question:

> **What event, reasoning or document fills this bracket?**

If the defence supplies it, the reconstruction changes.

If not, the competing explanation depends upon an invisible clinically material event.

## 15. Explanatory-model comparison

The repository should compare two models without assuming either one.

### Model A — ordinary longitudinal complexity

- genuine Parkinsonism;
- genuine structural foot disease;
- reasonable uncertainty about their relationship;
- appropriate Podiatry care;
- provisional PRKN hypothesis later losing favour;
- treatment responses recognised appropriately;
- diagnostic terminology evolving naturally;
- later reconsideration adequate;
- complaint/governance reliance on the record reasonable.

### Model B — founding partition and resilient frame

- a sophisticated neurological frame becomes authoritative before the neurological–foot relationship is adequately resolved;
- the unresolved foot is operationally allocated outside the principal frame;
- later neurological and mechanical evidence repeatedly crosses the boundary;
- the evidence is used locally without clear global reconstruction;
- specific historical propositions can be lost or broadened while global “Parkinson's” continuity survives;
- the accumulated record later supports retrospective validation and institutional reliance.

The analytical question is not which model sounds more accusatory.

It is:

> **Which model explains the documented sequence with fewer unsupported or undocumented transitions?**

This is the repository's preferred way to test the lock-in hypothesis.

## 16. Relationship to institutional parallax

The same unresolved clinical object changes institutional form:

> **clinical question → discharge uncertainty → specialty allocation → inherited clinical assumption → current-state treatment problem → retrospective continuity evidence → governance reliance.**

This is the practical face of **institutional parallax**.

The central distinction is:

> **the institutional meaning of the information can change even where the underlying embodied problem has not been resolved.**

Or:

> **The body remained unresolved while the institution progressively resolved the record.**

---

## 17. Relationship to documentary resilience

This reconstruction layer also sharpens the repository's resilience analysis.

Documentary resilience can arise not only through repetition, but through:

- inheritance of an authoritative founding synthesis;
- local use of new evidence without global reconstruction;
- retention of expert authority while diagnostic specificity is lost;
- movement to broader categories capable of preserving apparent continuity;
- and later institutional reliance on the accumulated record.

Thus the record need not be literally unchanged in order to appear continuous.

A more precise formulation is:

> **The record can remain globally coherent while material propositions underneath it change.**

The object-preservation layer adds a further mechanism: local categories can remain coherent while the relationship among them loses a clear owner. See [`INTEGRATIVE_OWNERSHIP_AND_OBJECT_PRESERVATION.md`](INTEGRATIVE_OWNERSHIP_AND_OBJECT_PRESERVATION.md).

## 18. What this layer is designed to achieve

This page should allow a reader to reach the structural hypothesis from the sources rather than being asked to accept **clinical lock-in** as a premise.

The ideal sequence is:

> **Here is what was known.**

> **Here is what remained unresolved.**

> **Here is the next observation.**

> **Here is what the next document did.**

> **Here is the transition the competing explanation requires.**

Then:

> **Which model explains the sequence?**

That moves the repo from advocacy-by-label to reconstruction-by-source.

The strongest methodological formulation is:

> **Do not ask any one document to prove the case. Ask what happens when the documents are allowed to speak to one another in sequence.**

## Evidential discipline

This file does **not** establish that:

- the founding partition was negligent;
- the foot pathology was wholly neurological;
- Podiatry was inappropriate;
- procyclidine or Sinemet proves a single mechanism;
- PRKN/PARK2 was genetically confirmed in 2017;
- idiopathic Parkinson's disease was medically impossible;
- the later diagnostic change was deliberate;
- Healy was consciously misrepresented;
- later clinicians failed to reconsider anything;
- the complaint or governance processes were invalid;
- or a missing transition proves concealment or bad faith.

It establishes a disciplined reconstruction method and a set of document-based transitions requiring explanation.

The shortest controlling formulation is:

> **Do not begin with lock-in. Reconstruct the information states. If the same unresolved proposition repeatedly survives evidence capable of bearing upon it without a visible integrative transition, the lock-in hypothesis becomes a conclusion to test rather than a premise to assume.**
