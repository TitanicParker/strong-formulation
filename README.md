# Strong Formulation

A structured evidential reconstruction of a longitudinal neurological record.

## The crystal-clear centre

The patient did **not** enter July 2017 with an already-recognised “unresolved foot relationship.” He entered with an **integrated phenotype**: painful abnormal feet, abnormal gait, pathological lower-limb tone, toe-clawing, impaired movement initiation and deteriorating function.

Neurology then identified severe very-young-onset Parkinsonism, a lower-limb appearance suggestive of dystonic posture, genuine structural foot disease and a possible PRKN/PARK2 phenotype. The surviving record identifies no substantial neurological remapping of the feet after those discoveries.

Shortly before discharge, after an eight-day admission, the patient directly said in substance that the feet had not been properly dealt with and that the neurological work could not therefore be complete. The neurologist answered **Podiatry**. There was no documented neurological reopening and no identified neurological handover carrying a defined feet–neurology question into Podiatry.

That is the hinge:

> **Before the Protest, the problem may be omission. After the Protest, it becomes decision.**

The discharge synthesis then did the work of partition. It preserved the dystonic-looking lower-limb sign, entertained PRKN/PARK2, preserved painful structural feet, but allocated the feet principally to a different explanatory route: possibly completely separate, possibly peripheral neuropathy, **“Not clear,”** and Podiatry. The patient’s earlier limited phenomenological distinction was strengthened into **“markedly divorced.”** The Protest itself did not enter the durable account. **“Plan/Recommendations: As per above”** then carried that constructed partition forward.

The Protest therefore did not challenge a separation the patient already understood. The patient challenged an **unfinished phenotype**. The documentary separation was the clinician-authored answer that followed.

> **The patient said the feet had not been investigated. The neurologist answered Podiatry. The discharge synthesis then supplied the intellectual architecture that made that answer look medically complete.**

## Why the later record matters

The founding synthesis did not lose the feet. It decided what explanatory pathway they were allowed to inhabit.

Later biology repeatedly crossed that boundary. Procyclidine brought gait, tension and feet together; withdrawal destabilised them together. In April 2018 the treating neurologist recorded that the first thing to change rapidly off procyclidine was the return of the outside-left-foot corn phenomenon. Podiatry independently documented a recurrent left fifth-MTPJ loading problem and repeatedly offloaded it mechanically. Primary care repeatedly regenerated the integrated formulation. Later dopaminergic treatment again altered foot pain.

The danger emerged because those facts were processed locally while the founding partition remained stable:

> **The biology updated. The document did not.**

> **Podiatry knew where it was. Neurology knew it changed with neurological treatment. The missing piece was the join.**

## Constructive knowledge

By documentary closure the hospital possessed, at minimum, an integrated referral state, severe very-young-onset Parkinsonism, a dystonic-looking lower-limb sign, painful structural feet, a possible PRKN/PARK2 phenotype, uncertainty about the feet, and a direct patient warning that the feet had not been properly worked through before discharge.

The hospital did not need to know the final mechanism. It knew enough to know that it did not know, and after the Protest it knew that the patient had specifically identified the incompleteness before closure.

## What the case does not require

It does not require proof that every structural foot abnormality had one neurological cause. It does not require proof that Neurology should have known the exact mechanism in July 2017. It does not require proof of private motive.

The narrower proposition is stronger: once the patient directly identified that the neurological work was unfinished, the clinician had to choose whether to reopen the question or proceed without doing so. The record shows the latter pathway, and the discharge synthesis made that pathway portable.

## Central evidential consequence

> **The hospital’s longitudinal record cannot serve as complete and self-sufficient proof of the material decision pathway because the durable continuity begins only after the patient’s direct warning that the phenotype was incompletely worked through had been omitted from the founding synthesis.**

And the shortest formulation remains:

> **The Protest broke the apparent completeness. The synthesis removed the break. The later record documented continuity across the gap.**
