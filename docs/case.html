10-minute route

One case, not 38 essays.

The question is not whether an ordinary record missed a detail. It is whether the document intended to preserve the material reasoning, patient understanding and forward plan omitted the direct challenge that most qualified those matters, then later acquired evidential authority as proof that they had been properly recorded.

The causal spine

integrated presentation→patient-derived separation strengthened→Protest→known contested uncertainty→still-open synthesis→directional omission→portable plan + compatible knowledge-state→later boundary tests→local updates, no identified global reconstruction→2025 challenge→historical continuity invoked
Aggravating factor 1

Omission from an intended authoritative account after direct notice

The Protest converted unresolved uncertainty into known contested uncertainty while the synthesis was still being formed. The final account preserved patient-state material that made the chosen plan appear cognitively coherent while omitting the patient state that directly challenged its adequacy.

Supportive patient-state information travelled; the decisive contrary patient-state did not.

The resulting document then became part of the portable historical account and later documentary continuity was invoked as evidence of reasoning and shared understanding. The omission therefore altered the evidential representation of the decision process itself.

Counsel formulation and four-part aggravation →

What the case establishes

The record entered with an integrated problem and an expressly unresolved relationship. Before closure, the patient protested the adequacy of the foot disposition. That Protest is an established case fact. The authoritative portable synthesis omitted it while preserving the selected branch and compatible knowledge-state. Later evidence repeatedly bore on the same boundary, and the 2025 response invoked documentary continuity and shared understanding.

What it does not require

It does not require proof that every structural lesion was caused by Parkinsonism, proof that every clinical entry was false, proof of a single exclusive mechanism, or proof of private motive.

The self-protecting documentary effect

The fact capable of challenging the documentary account was absent from the documentary account, and the documentary account was later used to prove that the historical process had been adequate. The Protest is therefore not expected to appear in the account whose omission is under examination; its absence there cannot be used to downgrade the event.
