# Counsel Handover

## Present assessment

The case is arguable but expert-dependent. The repository is not a pleading and should not be used to allege professional negligence against any identified practitioner without independent expert support and limitation review.

The most coherent negligence route is a defined failure of reassessment/integration/handover of the unresolved foot/gait/tone problem and/or a later failure to reconsider when materially new information emerged. The weakest route is to plead documentary language, diagnostic anchoring, later genetics or medication response as negligence by themselves.
