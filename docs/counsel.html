Counsel entry point

Material from which drafting can begin

The newest controlling proposition is not simply “an incomplete phenotype was contested.” The GP referral began with feet and gait; Neurology gave overwhelming weight to the striking neurological syndrome; after days of that work the patient restored the underweighted opening problem; the final synthesis then determined what that restored problem was allowed to mean.

Controlling hinge

The patient restores paragraph one of the referral.

The case does not require proof that the neurologist literally failed to read the referral. The record shows a marked asymmetry of clinical weighting. The neurological findings receive extensive examination, investigation, differential diagnosis, senior discussion and treatment planning. The painful abnormal feet through which the referral begins receive comparatively limited direct examination and no identified equivalent post-discovery remapping before closure.

After days of specialist work, the patient says in substance that the feet have not been properly dealt with and that Neurology cannot therefore be finished. That is not a new complaint. It restores the referrer’s original problem to the centre of the encounter.

Initial narrowing may be understandable. Once the patient restores the originating foot problem before closure, the whole referral is squarely back in play.

Five linked features

  1. Referral architecture: the opening paragraph begins with feet and gait; the remainder explains the neurological signs that make the whole presentation urgent.
  2. Asymmetric weighting: severe young-onset Parkinsonism, PRKN/PARK2, dystonic-looking gait and treatment strategy receive far greater elaboration than the painful feet.
  3. Direct restoration: before closure the patient expressly puts the underweighted opening problem back into play.
  4. Directional construction: the synthesis contains all major elements but assigns the painful feet to a separate/peripheral-neuropathy/Podiatry-compatible route ending “Not clear.”
  5. Later durability: subsequent mechanical and neurological observations repeatedly cross that boundary without an identified global reconstruction of the founding arrangement.

Professional-coherence pressure

By the time of the Protest, substantial diagnostic and therapeutic commitment has accumulated around severe young-onset Parkinsonism, possible PRKN/PARK2 and a cautious non-levodopa strategy involving senior Neurology. This is not proof of pride, vanity, embarrassment or bad faith.

The evidentially safer point is structural: reopening the restored first paragraph at that stage would require acknowledging that an elementary component of the referred problem remained insufficiently worked through despite days of sophisticated specialist work elsewhere.

Use “professional-coherence / commitment pressure” as an explanatory hypothesis, not as a proved motive.

What the synthesis actually does

It is not a document in which the relevant facts were never joined. In an important sense, the synthesis is the join. It preserves dystonic-looking lower-limb phenomenology, structural feet, possible PRKN/PARK2, possible neuropathy, the patient’s phenomenological distinction and the treatment strategy.

The issue is directional allocation: Parkinsonism/PRKN/treatment receive extensive explanatory force; the painful feet are made compatible with a separate/peripheral/Podiatry pathway; “markedly divorced” travels; the Protest does not; “As per above” makes the arrangement portable.

The discharge synthesis decides what the restored problem is allowed to mean.

Why reopening did not require levodopa

The patient’s objection did not require abandonment of Parkinsonism, PRKN reasoning or the cautious treatment strategy. Reassessment, phenotype mapping, multidisciplinary input, a defined neurological question to Podiatry, a return criterion or explicit preservation of the structural–dynamic issue were conceptually available while keeping the existing medication policy.

This matters because it separates the clinical completeness question from the separate expert question of whether levodopa should have been started in July 2017.

Later boundary-crossing evidence

Procyclidine response/withdrawal changed gait, tension and feet together. The April 2018 note records rapid return of an outside-left-foot phenomenon. Podiatry repeatedly documented and mechanically offloaded the recurrent fifth-MTPJ site. Later GP and dopaminergic evidence again crossed the founding boundary.

The later question is not simply “where was the join?” The founding synthesis already performed a join. The question is where its assigned relationships were globally reconstructed.

Issues requiring expert or production evidence

Clinical significance of the asymmetry; what reassessment or mapping would ordinarily be expected after the patient restored the original problem; significance of PRKN/PARK2 and dystonic-looking lower-limb signs; document drafting/finalisation chronology; any post-Protest reassessment; any defined neurological handover or return criterion; and any later document that globally reconstructs the founding arrangement.

Central evidential consequence

The founding synthesis is not merely evidence about the case; it is itself an evidential object requiring explanation. It was finalised after the patient restored the underweighted opening problem and it assigned the known facts the relationships and destinations that later readers inherited.

The patient did not discover a new problem at discharge. He restored the referrer’s original problem after days in which the record had overwhelmingly valued something else.
