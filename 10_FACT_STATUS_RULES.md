# Fact Status Rules

Every material proposition must carry one of these statuses.

## A — Primary contemporaneous record

A document/entry created in the ordinary course at or near the relevant event. It proves that the record says what it says; clinical truth and interpretation may still require expert evidence.

## B — Witness/patient factual evidence

A person's account of an event, including a later statement about an earlier event. It may prove the event if accepted, but must not be relabelled contemporaneous merely because it is stored in the canonical record.

## C — Derived factual reconstruction

Chronology, linkage or synthesis constructed from A/B material. It must identify its anchors and must not be cited as primary proof.

## D — Expert-dependent clinical inference

Mechanism, standard of care, diagnostic significance, counterfactual management, prognosis or causal attribution requiring appropriately qualified expert evidence.

## E — Legal proposition

Law derived from legislation/case authority. It must be current and separately cited; repository analysis is not legal authority.

## F — Advocacy / rhetorical formulation

Language designed to explain or argue the case: partition, inheritance, portable synthesis, commitment pressure, closure, documentary settlement and similar concepts. These may organise evidence but never prove A-D.

## Required notation

- If an original record is referenced but not reproduced sufficiently: `REQUIRES ORIGINAL DOCUMENT / PRODUCTION`.
- If a fact depends on the Protest: state whether it assumes the patient's account is accepted.
- If a proposition depends on a later event to criticise an earlier decision: separate hindsight evidence from what was knowable at the earlier date.
- If repeated wording is relied upon: identify the source text, later text, recipient and evidence of actual decision reliance.
- If causation is asserted: identify breach date, competent alternative, probable pathway and avoidable differential injury.

## Protest control

The 2025 Article 15 material is evidence that the patient gave that account in 2025. It is not a contemporaneous 2017 clinical note. The underlying 2017 event is a B-status proposition pending corroboration/adjudication.
