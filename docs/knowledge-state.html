The Patient's Documented Mind

Keep three things separate: what the patient said; what the clinician recorded the patient understood; and what that attributed understanding later did for the management account.

Founding mirror

Patient-origin position: the analytical reservoir characterises a limited distinction — “not the same problem.”

Authored state: “in his own mind — markedly divorced”.

Documentary effect: distinction → authored intensification → stronger support for separation.

The exact patient-origin wording is analytical unless independently anchored; the “markedly divorced” wording is primary clinician text at E005.

Contrary state

The Protest says the plan is incomplete because the presenting feet have not been adequately dealt with.

Documentary effect: the state supporting separation travels in the founding synthesis; the contrary state challenging closure does not.
