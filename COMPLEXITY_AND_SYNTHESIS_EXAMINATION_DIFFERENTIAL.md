# Complexity and the Synthesis–Examination Differential

**Status:** CONTROLLING EXPERT / DOCUMENTARY ANALYSIS — 9 September 2026

This file preserves a specific internal comparator within the July 2017 record. It is intended to answer, with evidential discipline, any broad contention that the unresolved neurological–foot relationship is adequately explained simply by the complexity of the presentation.

It does **not** assert that the case was clinically simple, that PRKN/PARK2 was wrongly entertained, or that negligence is proved. It identifies a disparity inside the hospital's own record that requires expert explanation.

---

## 1. Complexity is real, but the discharge synthesis supplies its own comparator

The July 2017 presentation was complex. Complexity can legitimately produce uncertainty.

But the discharge synthesis itself demonstrates that complexity did not prevent the department from constructing a sophisticated neurological phenotype.

The final synthesis was capable of selecting and combining features such as very young onset, akinetic-rigid Parkinsonism, symmetry, family-history pattern, cognitive preservation, treatment considerations and recessive inheritance reasoning into a serious PRKN/PARK2 hypothesis.

That creates an internal control:

> **If the phenotype was sufficiently intelligible to support sophisticated PRKN/PARK2 reasoning, what explains the much lower-resolution treatment of the recognised dystonic lower limb and painful abnormal foot?**

The defence cannot be tested only against an abstract proposition that the patient was complex. It can be tested against what the same department actually demonstrated itself capable of doing with the same presentation.

---

## 2. The synthesis–examination differential

The critical asymmetry is:

> **high-resolution retrospective phenotype construction versus low-resolution contemporaneous testing of the disputed lower-limb/foot relationship.**

The discharge synthesis appears to be more than a transcript of a single bedside encounter. It is a later clinical synthesis assembled from observation, investigation, discussion and reflection. That is ordinary medical practice.

But it means two things must be separated:

> **what was actually tested at the bedside**

from

> **what the department later inferred and assembled from selected features.**

The record appears capable of supporting the retrospective inference:

> **young onset + rigidity + bradykinesia + symmetry + family-history pattern + preserved cognition → PRKN/PARK2 plausibility.**

What is not equally visible is the return journey:

> **If PRKN/PARK2 is seriously entertained, what does that hypothesis require the clinician to ask again about dystonia, lower-limb posture, toe posture, the painful abnormal feet, gait, loading and the relationship between them?**

Thus:

> **The document may be phenotypically more complete on paper than the examination was in real time.**

Or, in the repository's established shorthand:

> **The synthesis may have outrun the examination.**

---

## 3. Why this weakens a generic complexity defence

A generic complexity defence would say, in substance, that the presentation was unusually difficult and that uncertainty about the feet was therefore unsurprising.

The internal record permits a more discriminating question:

> **Why was the neurological phenotype sufficiently interpretable to support sophisticated PRKN/PARK2 reasoning, while the relationship between a recognised dystonic-looking lower limb and the painful abnormal foot remained insufficiently interrogated to move beyond “Not clear”?**

That converts the issue from mere difficulty into a question about **selectivity of inquiry**.

The safe proposition is:

> **The sophistication of the final neurological synthesis makes the relative absence of documented interrogation of the disputed lower-limb/foot relationship more, not less, significant.**

This is not a claim that every element of a complex phenotype must be solved before discharge. It is a question about whether the unresolved feature that sat directly beside the suspected movement disorder received a level of phenotypic inquiry proportionate to the sophistication being applied elsewhere.

---

## 4. The left foot outside a strong frame

The neurological frame was not weak.

It was strong enough to organise severe young-onset Parkinsonism, dystonic-looking gait, recessive inheritance reasoning, PRKN/PARK2 and treatment strategy.

The painful left foot was not absent from the record. It was visible, structurally abnormal and part of the presenting problem. Yet its relationship to the recognised lower-limb motor abnormality remained **“Not clear”** and became operationally capable of being treated as separate.

The human formulation is:

> **The left foot was caught outside a very strong frame.**

The more formal formulation is:

> **A clinically important part of the phenotype may have been left outside an explanatory structure strong enough to organise almost everything that followed.**

That is why later continuity matters. A strong founding frame can allow later information about the excluded feature to be recognised, treated and documented without necessarily forcing the frame itself to be reconstructed.

---

## 5. Professional closure before clinical completion

The alleged founding problem can therefore be stated as:

> **professional closure before clinical completion of the neurological–foot phenotype.**

Complex cases do not have to be fully solved before discharge. But if an important relationship remains unresolved, reasonable care may require that the relationship remain clinically open, be expressly investigated, or be transferred through a defined process capable of resolving it.

If the patient's account of the Protest is proved, the issue becomes sharper: the patient expressly identified before discharge that the feet had not been dealt with, while the service still possessed the opportunity and expertise to reopen the relationship.

The controlling conditional formulation is:

> **The service may have completed the professional act of discharge before completing the clinical act of integration.**

And the resulting asymmetry is striking:

> **the institution retained the expertise; the patient carried away the unresolved phenotype.**

> **the patient left with the problem still embodied; the record left with the problem already partitioned.**

---

## 6. The expert question that complexity cannot answer by itself

The strongest neutral expert question is:

> **Given the level of phenotypic sophistication demonstrated in the discharge synthesis, including serious PRKN/PARK2 reasoning, was the documented assessment of the dystonic lower-limb/foot relationship proportionate to the clinical significance of that feature? If not, what further examination, synthesis or follow-up would reasonable neurological practice have required?**

A sharper cross-examination form is:

> **If the case was sufficiently intelligible to construct PRKN/PARK2, why was it too complex to interrogate the foot phenotype that was already abnormal and dystonic-looking?**

If the Protest is proved:

> **Once the patient expressly identified that unresolved part of the phenotype before discharge, what remaining complexity justified closure rather than returning to it?**

The purpose of these questions is not rhetoric. It is to force comparison between two evidential states generated by the same department:

> **what the final synthesis knew** versus **what the surviving bedside record shows was actually tested.**

---

## 7. Why the disparity is clinically striking

The repository should preserve the point without overstatement:

> **A service sophisticated enough to construct an uncommon genetic Parkinsonism phenotype from the admission could apparently leave the painful foot outside that phenotype without the surviving record demonstrating an equivalently sophisticated attempt to determine whether the recognised dystonic lower limb and abnormal gait were mechanically or neurologically connected to it.**

The appropriate evidential phrase is:

> **The disparity is clinically striking and requires explanation.**

The rhetorical shorthand “extraordinary” may describe the apparent disparity, but expert analysis should identify exactly what examination, synthesis or follow-up was required and whether its absence fell below reasonable practice.

---

## 8. Interaction with documentary resilience

This differential matters beyond July 2017 because the sophisticated synthesis became the founding information state.

If its theoretical sophistication exceeded the clinical work demonstrated beneath the disputed foot relationship, later readers could inherit an appearance of comprehensiveness that the underlying bedside record did not fully demonstrate.

That links directly to [`DOCUMENTARY_RESILIENCE_CONTINUITY_AND_RELIANCE.md`](DOCUMENTARY_RESILIENCE_CONTINUITY_AND_RELIANCE.md):

> **a synthesis may have outrun the examination → the synthesis structures the longitudinal record → downstream continuity accumulates within that frame → later review relies on the accumulated record → the founding frame acquires additional authority.**

Accordingly, complexity and documentary resilience are not separate themes. The sophistication of the discharge document is both the answer to a generic complexity defence and the reason later reliance on that document requires careful provenance analysis.

---

## 9. Controlling formulation

> **The case cannot be answered simply by saying that the presentation was complex. The hospital's own discharge synthesis demonstrates that the department was capable of sophisticated retrospective phenotypic construction, including serious PRKN/PARK2 reasoning. The expert question is why that sophistication is not matched in the surviving record by an equivalent interrogation of the recognised dystonic lower limb and painful abnormal foot before their relationship was left “Not clear” and operationally partitioned. The complexity of the case therefore does not remove the issue; it creates an internal comparator against which the selectivity and adequacy of the inquiry can be tested.**

Shortest form:

> **Complexity explains uncertainty only if the uncertainty reflects the limits of adequate inquiry. It does not explain why sophisticated reasoning was possible inside the frame while the disputed foot relationship remained comparatively untested outside it.**

## Evidential discipline

This file does **not** establish that:

- PRKN/PARK2 was wrongly considered;
- the entire phenotype was clinically obvious;
- all relevant examination was necessarily documented;
- the foot disorder was wholly neurological;
- complexity was irrelevant;
- or breach is proved.

It establishes an internal differential that should be put squarely to independent expert evidence: **the sophistication of the final synthesis versus the clinical work demonstrated in relation to the feature that was partitioned outside it.**
