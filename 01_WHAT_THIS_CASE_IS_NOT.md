# What This Case Is Not

This control prevents analytical propositions from being promoted into facts or causes of action.

- A later PRKN/genetic result does not itself prove negligence in 2017.
- A later response to levodopa/Sinemet does not itself prove what the mechanism was or what treatment was required in 2017.
- A clinician's diagnostic uncertainty is not itself negligence.
- 'Documentary partition', 'portable synthesis', 'inheritance', 'commitment pressure' and similar terms are analytical descriptions, not freestanding Irish torts.
- Repeated wording does not prove that a later clinician relied on it in making a decision; reliance must be evidenced.
- An omission from a discharge letter does not itself prove that the omitted event did not occur, nor that its omission caused injury.
- The Protest is not presently established by a contemporaneous 2017 note. The present repository anchor is a later patient account of the alleged 2017 interaction. The event may be proved by admissible testimony and corroboration, but its status must remain explicit.
- GDPR accuracy/rectification is not a mechanism for having a regulator decide that a bona fide historical clinical opinion was medically wrong.
- HSE complaints procedures do not adjudicate matters that fall solely within clinical judgment; administrative handling and incomplete/erroneous information remain analytically separate.
- Professional guidance informs expert/regulatory analysis but does not automatically establish negligence.
- The case is not proved until breach, factual causation, legal causation and compensable damage are established for a defined defendant and date.
