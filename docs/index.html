A forensic model of referral weighting, restoration and record construction

The patient restored the referrer’s original problem. The synthesis decided what it was allowed to mean.

The neurological emphasis may initially have been understandable. The decisive event came after days of specialist work, when the patient put the first paragraph of the GP referral squarely back into view before closure.

Six steps

1

Integrated referral

The GP begins with bilateral foot problems, callouses/corns and abnormal gait, then explains the pathological tone, initiation difficulty, toe-clawing, dexterity loss and functional decline that make the whole presentation neurological.

2

Asymmetric weighting

Neurology develops the severe young-onset Parkinsonian syndrome, dystonic-looking gait, PRKN/PARK2 reasoning and treatment strategy far more extensively than the painful abnormal feet through which the referral began.

3

Referral restored

After days of specialist work, the patient says in substance that the feet have not been properly dealt with and the work cannot be complete. He is not adding a new problem; he is restoring paragraph one.

4

Commitment pressure

By then diagnostic, hierarchical and therapeutic investment has accumulated. This does not prove pride, but it creates professional-coherence pressure if the admission must now acknowledge an elementary unfinished component.

5

Directional synthesis

The final synthesis contains all major elements but arranges them so the feet can travel as separate / possibly neuropathic / “Not clear” / Podiatry while the Parkinsonism architecture remains complete.

6

Durable behaviour

Later biology and mechanics repeatedly cross the founding boundary, but the new facts are largely absorbed locally without an identified global reconstruction of the relationships assigned in 2017.

Why the moment matters

The case does not depend on showing that the neurologist literally failed to read the referral. The record itself shows asymmetric clinical weighting. Once the patient expressly restored the underweighted opening problem, the whole referral was back in play before closure.

1

Referral architecture

Feet and gait are not incidental history. They are the concrete problem through which Brennan’s referral begins; the neurological signs explain why he asks Neurology to solve the whole presentation.

2

Direct restoration

The patient’s NCT / bald-tyres objection is an inquiry-completeness objection: you have done the neurological work, but the thing I came in with remains insufficiently dealt with.

3

Documentary answer

The final synthesis is not missing the join. It is the join. It decides the explanatory pathway each retained fact will occupy and makes that arrangement portable through “As per above.”

The patient did not discover a new problem at discharge. He restored the referrer’s original problem after days in which the record had overwhelmingly valued something else. The discharge synthesis then had to decide what that restored problem was allowed to mean.

The record’s behaviour

The record does not generally lose difficult facts. It contains them, assigns them status, gives them destinations and controls the relationships among them.

Integrated information enters → one pathway is amplified → other facts remain present but underdeveloped → relationships are assigned → uncertainty receives a destination → prose becomes plan → later crossing evidence is absorbed locally.

The biology updated. The founding arrangement remained stable.

Primary anchors beneath the proposition

Evidence view exposes source status and `CLIN-` ranges. The central distinction is between what the record proves — referral architecture, asymmetric elaboration, the Protest account, and the final synthesis — and what remains an explanatory inference, including professional-coherence or commitment pressure.
