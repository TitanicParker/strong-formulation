These are provenance questions. The repository should distinguish them from retrospective speculation about what the review process "must have" contained.

# 4. Article 16 completion request served on GUH — 2 September 2026

A separate Article 16 request was sent to the GUH Data Protection Officer on 2 September 2026.

This request is deliberately different from the Article 15 provenance request.

It asks GUH to complete the retained July 2017 personal-data record by means of a **supplementary statement**.

The data subject expressly does **not** ask GUH to delete, alter or retrospectively rewrite the historical clinician-authored record, diagnosis, opinion or reasoning.

The requested operation is:

> **retain the original July 2017 record intact, but durably attach or directly cross-reference the patient's supplementary statement so that its existence is apparent whenever the July 2017 synthesis is subsequently accessed, reviewed, relied upon, disclosed or otherwise processed.**

The request asks GUH, if it acts on the request, to confirm:

- the date completion was made;
- where and how the supplementary statement is recorded;
- how it is linked or cross-referenced to the July 2017 synthesis;
- whether the 2017 information object itself carries an indication that supplementary information exists; and
- where applicable, how communication to recipients is dealt with under Article 19 GDPR.

If GUH declines, the request asks for the reasons and for clarity as to the controller's position on incompleteness, supplementary completion, or any other legal basis relied upon.

# 5. Content of the attached supplementary statement

The attached two-page statement is dated **2 September 2026**.

Its purpose is narrowly defined.

It records the patient's account that on **10 July 2017**, after learning that discharge would occur the following day, he caused the treating neurologist to be summoned because he believed the presenting painful-foot problem had not been adequately returned to.

It records the NCT / bald-tyres Protest and the recalled response directing the matter toward Podiatry.

It also records that the patient does not recall a further focused neurological examination or reassessment of the relationship between the painful feet and the neurological lower-limb abnormalities before discharge.

The statement explains why the patient considers the existing synthesis incomplete: the surviving document contains extensive data concerning his questions, understanding, debriefing, the "clear plan" and the disposition of the feet, but does not record the pre-discharge interaction in which he expressly challenged the adequacy of that disposition.

The statement is careful about scope. It does not ask GUH to adopt the patient's interpretation of the clinical significance of the event. It asks that **the patient's own account of the omitted interaction travel with the synthesis to which it relates**.

That is precisely why this request matters to the documentary case.

The historical issue is omission from the 2017 portable account. The Article 16 request seeks to prevent that omission from continuing to govern future processing of the same historical information object without visible qualification.

### Important evidential distinction

If GUH grants the Article 16 request, that would create a **current/prospective completion state**.

It would **not** retrospectively prove that the supplementary statement was part of the 2017 record, nor would it erase the historical fact that the founding synthesis originally travelled without it.

The repository must preserve that distinction.

# 8. What must not be inferred while the requests are pending

The present waiting state should be kept evidentially clean.

The repository should **not** infer that:

- requested provenance data exist merely because they were requested;
- provenance data do not exist merely because they have not yet been supplied;
- silence means refusal;
- the Article 16 supplementary statement has been accepted or linked before GUH confirms the operation;
- the DPC has enlarged complaint C-25-7-2074 to include the new Article 15 / Article 16 issues;
- absence of an earlier version proves no earlier version existed;
- absence of an audit event proves an event did not occur unless the system's audit characteristics support that inference; or
- a 2026 completion of the record alters the historical state of the 2017 synthesis as originally created and transmitted.

The correct categories on receipt are:

> **held and supplied**  
> **held but withheld / partially supplied**  
> **not held**  
> **no longer held**  
> **not answered / unclear**

Those categories must not be collapsed into one another.
