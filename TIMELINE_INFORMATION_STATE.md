# Timeline — Referral Return, Directional Synthesis, Re-joining and the 2023 Convergence Crisis

This timeline follows the information state in the corrected order. The 2017 discharge synthesis is treated as the **founding join**: it contains structural, dystonic, Parkinsonian and PRKN material and arranges those elements directionally. The later problem is not that no join ever existed; it is that later mechanical and treatment-state evidence repeatedly crossed the founding arrangement without an identified global **re-join / reconstruction**. The later record also now reveals a second question in 2023: whether a severe pain, mobility, instability and psychological-endurance crisis was integrated as one contemporaneous safety object.

> **Integrated GP referral → understandable specialist concentration on the striking neurological syndrome → asymmetric clinical weighting → patient restores paragraph one before closure → answer returns to pre-existing Podiatry pathway / no identified new neurological handover question → discharge synthesis joins the known elements directionally into Parkinsonism/PRKN versus separate-or-neuropathic “Not clear” feet → Protest omitted → “As per above” makes the arrangement portable → later mechanical and pharmacological evidence repeatedly crosses the boundary → genuine current-state reopening without identified global historical re-join / reconstruction.**

## The second 2023 spine

> **Known gait/balance vulnerability → urgent severe pain crisis with death/endurance language → maximal pregabalin exposure and major functional restriction → current-state Neurology reopening → directly observed stumbles → extensive branch-specific diagnostic/medication reasoning → no identified falls-focused response or explicit whole-risk integration in the patient-facing consultation.**

This second spine must remain analytically separate from the 2017 founding-negligence question until expert evidence establishes what response should ordinarily have followed.

## The corrected join proposition

> **The founding join existed: it was the discharge synthesis. The later missing operation was the re-join — the explicit reconstruction of that synthesis when new mechanical and neurological treatment-state evidence crossed its boundaries.**

## The longitudinal safety proposition
