# Timeline — Referral Return, Directional Synthesis and Later Re-joining Failure

This timeline follows the information state in the corrected order. The key advance is that the 2017 discharge synthesis is treated as the **founding join**: it contains the structural, dystonic, Parkinsonian and PRKN material and arranges those elements directionally. The later problem is not that no join ever existed; it is that later mechanical and treatment-state evidence repeatedly crossed the founding arrangement without an identified global **re-join / reconstruction**.

## The corrected causal spine

> **Integrated GP referral → understandable specialist concentration on the striking neurological syndrome → asymmetric clinical weighting → patient restores paragraph one before closure → Podiatry answer / no identified neurological reopening → discharge synthesis joins the known elements directionally into Parkinsonism/PRKN versus separate-or-neuropathic “Not clear” feet → Protest omitted → “As per above” makes the arrangement portable → later mechanical and pharmacological evidence repeatedly crosses the boundary → local updates without identified global re-join / reconstruction.**

## The corrected join proposition

> **The founding join existed: it was the discharge synthesis. The later missing operation was the re-join — the explicit reconstruction of that synthesis when new mechanical and neurological treatment-state evidence crossed its boundaries.**

## The longitudinal safety proposition

> **The biology updated. The documentary architecture did not visibly reconstruct itself around those updates.**
