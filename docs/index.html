The Protest occurred before closure. The founding portable account omitted it.

The aggravating feature is not simply that the Protest is missing. The document later relied upon to prove the decision process omitted the event that most directly qualified that process.

The new information-state formulation

The case now accepts that “Not clear” could have been legitimate clinical uncertainty in July 2017. The sharper question is why that uncertainty was finalized without a defined resolution mechanism after the patient had directly protested the foot disposition, and why the same unresolved state survived repeated later evidence capable of testing it.

GP

Sent one integrated gait–feet–tone problem and received a specialist account that left the relationship neurologically possible but unresolved.

Podiatry

Repeatedly localised and offloaded the left fifth-MTPJ problem, generating a precise mechanical/structural stream.

Neurology

Separately documented that the outside-left-foot phenomenon changed rapidly with neurological treatment state.

Podiatry knew where it was. Neurology knew it changed with neurological treatment. The record does not identify the point at which those facts were formally joined.

The missing decision fork

The actual decision history contains an established material contest before closure. The portable history omits it. The absence is therefore not a reason to doubt the Protest; it is the documentary defect the repository examines.

Primary anchors beneath the proposition

Evidence view exposes source status and `CLIN-` ranges for the documentary record. The Protest itself is an established case fact; audit metadata must not convert its omission from the portable account into a challenge to whether it occurred.

The shortest formulation

The Protest broke the shared understanding. The synthesis omitted the break. The later record documented continuity across the gap.

By 2025 the clinical facts had moved. The information state had not.
