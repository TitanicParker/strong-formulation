Method

Persuasive because auditable

Claim typing

Derived claims are tagged as fact, clinician assertion, patient-state, documentary analysis, record-audit inference or audit question. Analysis is not silently promoted into primary fact.

The Protest

The Protest is an established case fact. It occurred before closure and directly challenged the adequacy of the foot disposition. Its absence from the final portable founding account is therefore not a provenance weakness: that omission is the central documentary fact under examination.

Omission discipline

The repository does not reason that “not recorded” means “did not occur.” It asks the opposite record-audit question: once a material contest is established to have occurred before closure, what did the portable synthesis preserve, and what did it omit?

Negative proof discipline

No allegation of private motive is required. Failure to prove one exclusive mechanism is not proof that separation was reasonable. Absence of an identified reconstructive document remains an audit question open to later production.

Privacy

The canonical forensic file is preserved unchanged. Pages publishes only minimally necessary extracts and derived data. Direct identifiers are excluded from the generated site artifact.

Validation and build

`scripts/validate.py` checks the derived data and source anchors. `scripts/build_site.py` creates `_site/` from `docs/` and `data/` only; the canonical medical transcription is not copied into the Pages artifact.
